## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Mostly clear of the AI-design-system-page reflex. The lowercase wordmark, monospace section numbers, dashed-rule dividers, repeating-stripe halftone placeholders, and the closing footer copy push it away from the saturated lane. BUT three explicit absolute-ban violations are present, and one is on the very first content surface.

**Deterministic scan:** `npx impeccable detect --json` over the TSX files returned `[]` (clean). The detector does not scan CSS-only files; all three absolute-ban violations below live in `poster.css`.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely strong specimen at 33/40, undermined by three rule-breaking surfaces that the document itself catalogs as forbidden three sections later. The single biggest opportunity: make the poster obey its own rules. Fix that and 33 becomes 37+.

## What's Working

1. **The spec-as-caption pattern.** Every specimen carries its own JetBrains Mono readout — `200ms standard`, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)`, `state · default`. The rule lives next to the example.
2. **The empty-state specimen** (`page.tsx:2178–2214`) — open magnifying glass with one teal accent stroke, four category chips, two escape CTAs, zero apology.
3. **The Don'ts gallery in §09.4** (`page.tsx:2040–2069`) — teaches by counter-example with named failures. Confident, specific.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Hero brand card uses a teal-to-darker-teal gradient
- **Why it matters:** Violates the gradient ban on the first content surface. Pushes the wordmark into SaaS/AI register where the poster needs "public infrastructure."
- **Where:** `poster.css:222–226` (`.brand-card.hero`)
- **Fix:** Flat ink (`var(--ink)`) or flat deep teal. If depth is wanted, `var(--shadow-card)` against sand, not an internal gradient.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

### [P0] Glassmorphism on the product card bookmark
- **Why it matters:** `backdrop-filter: blur(4px) + rgba(255,255,255,0.9)` is textbook glassmorphism, banned in the system rules. It's the default state on every product card across §06 and §11.
- **Where:** `poster.css:1107–1122` (`.product .bookmark`)
- **Fix:** Solid `var(--white)` background, 1px `var(--cloud-2)` border, no blur. Or move the bookmark out of the image well.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

### [P0] Em-dash habit violates the system's own copy rule
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md bans em dashes; the poster uses 40+ in user-facing strings, including the lede, most section descriptions, and the Don'ts list itself (which catalogs banned patterns while using the banned dash).
- **Where:** `page.tsx` throughout — grep `&mdash;` and `—`.
- **Fix:** Mechanical pass: replace with commas, periods, colons, or parentheticals.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Sample-composition placeholder thumb uses a smooth gradient
- **Why it matters:** `.m-thumb` is the one placeholder on the page using a smooth two-color gradient instead of the documentary repeating-stripe halftone used everywhere else.
- **Where:** `poster.css:1633`
- **Fix:** Swap for the repeating-linear-gradient halftone used by `.product .img .ph-art` at line 1104.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] No active-section indicator on the TOC, no back-to-top
- **Why it matters:** All 11 TOC anchors look identical regardless of scroll position. After §11 there's no escape.
- **Where:** `poster.css:2412–2436` (`.toc`); footer at `page.tsx:2354–2381`.
- **Fix:** IntersectionObserver-driven `.is-active` class; add `↑ Top` in the footer-right slot. ~30 LOC.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P3] One-Voice budget: teal concentrated in the type grid
- **Why it matters:** Every `.font-tag` is teal — 11 rows produce a teal column running down the right side of the type grid.
- **Where:** `poster.css:443–447`
- **Fix:** Drop `.font-tag` to `var(--slate)` for static spec labels.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize` (with intent to *reduce* color)

## Minor Observations

- `#fff` is used as text color (not surface) on dark backgrounds. Rule is about surfaces; this is fine.
- `.brand-card.hero .eyebrow { color: #8dd5d8 }` is an off-palette tint chosen for the dark hero. Once the hero gradient goes, resolve to `var(--teal-light)`.
- Type grid row 9 specs a 13px uppercase "Badge label" — violates the Caps Ceiling Rule (12px max). The rendered `.tb` badges correctly use 12px; the spec disagrees with itself.
- `.canvas` (sample-composition frame, line 2337) is sand-on-sand with a single hairline — reads as a continuation of the page. Slightly deeper inset would land the frame.
- `.bp .ownership` chip sits adjacent to trust badges and reads as a fourth trust-tier color. Different shape would separate it.
- Repeating-stripe halftones all share 135° angle and produce a faint Moiré in stacks of four. Vary angles per category.
