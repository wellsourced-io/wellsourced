## Principles

This design system exists to keep WellSourced **coherent with its values**. Trust is earned in detail — a hundred small moments of clarity add up to one trustworthy product. The guidelines below are how we hold that line.

- **Clarity over cleverness.** A direct sentence beats a clever one. A plain button beats a novel one.
- **Honesty over hype.** We show what's verified, and we say what isn't. Badges are calibrated, not motivational.
- **Warmth without cuteness.** Rounded UI, approachable language — but never twee, never sing-songy.
- **Convenience first.** Prices are visible. Buy-direct links are prominent. Friction is for what's earned, not what's expected.
- **Institutional trust.** Deep teal, clean sans-serif, visible verification. Feels like it could be governmental in the best sense.
